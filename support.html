Common troubleshooting

The scholarship list is not loading

Confirm that you have internet access for online content, then close and reopen the app. Locally bundled or previously saved information may still remain available.

A scholarship link does not open

The provider may have changed or removed its page. Please report the scholarship title and the broken link to support.

I am not receiving reminders

Open iPhone Settings, select Scholarship Finder, then confirm notifications are permitted. Also verify that the reminder date has not passed.

My saved information disappeared

Core data is stored locally. Deleting the app, resetting the device, or restoring without the relevant app data may remove locally stored information.

Response expectations

We aim to review support inquiries as promptly as possible. Do not send Social Security numbers, financial account information, passwords, or other highly sensitive personal information by email.
