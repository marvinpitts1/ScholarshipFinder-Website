About us

Education should remain possible at every stage of life.

Scholarship Finder was created to make funding opportunities easier to discover and manage for people balancing education with work, family, service, and life transitions.

Our mission

Our mission is to help learners find relevant scholarship opportunities, understand key requirements, and organize the application process with greater confidence.

Who we serve

Scholarship Finder is especially designed with adult learners, returning students, career changers, parents, veterans, community-college students, graduate students, and lifelong learners in mind. The app may also be useful to any student seeking a clearer way to organize scholarship research.

Our approach

We prioritize clear information, official application sources, practical organization, and respectful guidance. Scholarship information can change, so users should always confirm deadlines, eligibility, award amounts, and requirements directly with the scholarship provider before applying.

Our values

Opportunity

Education can open new paths at any age.

Clarity

Complex requirements should be easier to understand.

Privacy

Personal information should remain under the user’s control.

Responsibility

Technology should support—not replace—careful judgment.
