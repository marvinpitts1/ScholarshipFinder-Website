Frequently asked questions

Helpful answers before you begin.

What is Scholarship Finder?

Scholarship Finder is an iPhone app that helps users discover scholarship opportunities, save favorites, and organize applications.

Who is the app designed for?

The app is especially designed for adult learners, returning students, career changers, parents, veterans, and lifelong learners, while remaining useful to a broader student audience.

Is an account required?

No account is currently required for the app’s core features.

Does Scholarship Finder award scholarships?

No. Scholarship Finder is an independent discovery and organization tool. Scholarship providers make all eligibility and award decisions.

Is scholarship information guaranteed to be current?

No. We work to present useful information, but providers can change deadlines, requirements, amounts, and availability. Always verify details on the official provider website.

Does the AI Assistant guarantee that I qualify or will win?

No. The assistant provides educational planning and organizational guidance only. It does not make admissions, legal, financial-aid, or award decisions.

Can I use the app offline?

Core locally stored information may remain available offline. Opening provider websites and retrieving future online updates requires internet access.

How do I request support?

Email or visit the Support page.
